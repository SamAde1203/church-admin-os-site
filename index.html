Questions pastors ask

Do I need a paid Notion plan?

No. Church Admin OS works on Notion's free plan. Larger teams may want a paid plan for more guest invites and page-level permissions, and the setup guide explains when it's worth it.

How long does it take to set up?

About 30 minutes for the basics. The setup guide walks you through clearing the demo data, adding your first Sunday and inviting volunteers.

Is our pastoral data safe?

The template includes a UK GDPR setup guide. It shows you how to keep care notes in a staff-only area so volunteers only ever see their own rota.

Can volunteers use it on their phones?

Yes. The Volunteer Hub works in the free Notion mobile app. Volunteers tap a link to see their shifts and can flag when they need cover.

We're a small church. Is this too much?

Start with the Starter edition and only the parts you need. Everything is connected, so you can grow into the full system later.

Can you set it up for us?

Yes. The Done-for-you package includes installation, customisation, importing your existing lists and live training for your team.
